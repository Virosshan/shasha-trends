Size guide Sample

Measurements are of the garment, in inches, and may differ slightly by style. Between sizes? Take the larger for an easier fit.

How to measure

  • Bust — around the fullest part, tape level under the arms.
  • Waist — around the narrowest part of your torso.
  • Hip — around the fullest part of your hips.
  • Kurti length — highest shoulder point to the hem.
